Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

BetaWeather ("the app") is a weather and conditions tool for rock climbers and boulderers. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected.

1. Information We Collect

BetaWeather does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, or identifier is ever requested.
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting services are integrated.
  • No advertising SDKs are included.

2. Location Data

The app may request access to your device's location (while the app is in use) solely to center the map on your current position and to show nearby climbing areas.

  • Your location is used on-device only and is never sent to any server operated by BetaWeather.
  • Location data is not stored, logged, or shared with third parties.
  • You can deny the location permission at any time in your device settings. The app will continue to work; the map will simply not auto-center.

3. Weather Data

Weather forecasts are fetched from Open-Meteo, a free and open-source weather API. The only data sent to Open-Meteo is the geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of the climbing area you are viewing — no personal information is included in those requests.

Open-Meteo's own privacy policy is available at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

4. Local Storage

The app stores your saved (favourite) climbing areas locally on your device using the standard Android shared preferences mechanism. This data never leaves your device.

5. Third-Party Services

BetaWeather uses one external service:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecast data. Coordinates of the selected climbing area are sent to retrieve forecast data. No personal data is transmitted.

No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are used.

6. Children's Privacy

BetaWeather does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
