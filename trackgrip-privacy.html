Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

TrackGrip ("the app") is a training app for climbers that connects to a Bluetooth grip-strength sensor. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. Account & Sign-In

You can create an account with Google Sign-In or, on iPhone, with Sign in with Apple. With Google, Google shares your email address, name, and profile photo with the app via Firebase Authentication. With Apple, Apple shares your name and email address; if you choose "Hide My Email", the app only receives an anonymous relay address from Apple. The app itself only stores your internal account ID (UID) to link your data to your account — your email, name, and photo are not copied into the app's database beyond what Firebase Authentication manages for the sign-in session itself.

2. Training & Grip-Strength Data

When you use the app, the following is stored under your account so it is available across your devices:

  • Computed grip-strength results (e.g. max force, crimp/pinch strength) per hand
  • Your training profile (name, age, gender, climbing grades) if you choose to fill it in
  • Workout history (which workouts you completed, duration, peak force, date)
  • Achievements, streaks, and progress statistics

This data is private to your account and is not visible to other users.

3. Community Features (Optional)

TrackGrip has optional community features you can choose to take part in:

  • Leaderboard — if you submit your best grip-strength value, your chosen nickname and that value become visible to other users. This only happens if you actively submit it.
  • Shared training plans — if you upload a training plan to the community, its content (title, description, exercises) and your nickname become visible to other users. You can delete plans you've uploaded at any time from within the app.

Nothing is shared publicly unless you take an explicit action to share it.

3a. Reporting, Hiding & Blocking

You can report a community training plan that you consider spam, offensive, or dangerous. A report stores your account ID (UID), the reported plan and its author's account ID, the reason you selected, an optional note you write, and the time of the report. Reports are used only to review and, if necessary, remove content, and are kept only as long as needed to handle the report and prevent abuse. A plan that receives several reports is hidden automatically until it has been reviewed.

If you hide a plan or block a user, this list is stored only on your device and is not shared with anyone, including the blocked user.

4. Bluetooth Sensor Data

TrackGrip connects to a dedicated Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) grip-strength sensor to read live force measurements during a session.

  • Raw, live sensor readings are processed only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Only the computed results of a session (e.g. your peak force for that session) are saved to your account, as described in Section 2.
  • The app does not require or request access to any other Bluetooth devices.

5. Local Storage

The app caches your data (profile, results, workout history, app preferences such as theme and language) locally on your device using standard local storage, in addition to syncing it to your account. This lets the app work smoothly and load quickly; it does not add any additional data collection beyond what's described above.

6. Data Export

TrackGrip has an optional "Export" feature that writes your grip-strength results to a file on your own device, so a separate app you use ("ClimbingApp") can read it. This file is written locally only — TrackGrip does not transmit it anywhere itself.

7. Third-Party Services

TrackGrip uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Functions, App Check) to provide account sign-in, cloud storage, and abuse protection. Firebase acts strictly as our data processor/infrastructure provider.

  • No advertising SDKs are included.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included.
  • No user data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

8. Deleting Your Account & Data

In the app: go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your training data, profile, workout history, leaderboard entry, and uploaded community plans, and deletes your account sign-in itself. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID. This cannot be undone.

Without the app: if you no longer have the app installed, email trackyourgrip@gmail.com from the address associated with your Google or Apple account and request account deletion. We will delete your data within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

TrackGrip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
